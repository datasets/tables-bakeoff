Which JavaScript table library should you use?

It depends what the table is for. We built the same table nine ways with 1,000 to 500,000 rows of UK house sales, timed each, and checked what each can do. Three jobs, and what we would use for each.

The jobUseRunner-upWhy
A table on a pageReport, article, docs. Up to a few thousand rows. Plain <table> Observable Inputs, one call, sort on click 39 lines, 1 kB, 1,000 rows in about 55 ms. Prints, copies, Ctrl-F finds every row. Want a search box? DataTables.
A big gridTens of thousands to 500,000 rows. Sort and filter built in. Any framework. AG Grid Community RevoGrid, 62 ms at 500,000 rows, with a corner link 500,000 rows in 233 ms at 60 fps, 40 lines. Fails loudly when you misconfigure it. 333 kB; clipboard and grouping are Enterprise.
An editable gridEdit cells, paste ranges, spreadsheet feel. Tabulator RevoGrid, passed with no config Edit and paste both passed our test with options from its own docs. Free, MIT, no framework. Give it a parent with a height.
Build your ownReact, your markup and CSS. TanStack Table + virtualizer 24 kB over React, 150 lines you write. What DataHub uses.
